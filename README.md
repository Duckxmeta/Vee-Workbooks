## 📲 How to Download to Any Device

### On Desktop (Mac / Windows / Linux)
1. Click any **`📥 Direct Download`** link in the table above.
2. Your browser will automatically save the PDF to your **Downloads** folder.
3. Alternatively, click **`👁️ Read Online`**, then click the download icon in the top-right corner of GitHub's PDF viewer.

### On Mobile (iPhone / iPad / Android)
1. Tap any **`📥 Direct Download`** link.
2. Select **"Save to Files"** (iOS) or **"Download"** (Android).
3. Open in your favorite reader app (Apple Books, Files, PDF Expert, Drive).
